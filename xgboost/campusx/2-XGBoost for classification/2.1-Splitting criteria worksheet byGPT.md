# 🏗️ XGBoost Splitting Criteria Example

This example walks thru' using the toy dataset on how to determine the best split at the root node.

---

# 📋 1. Dataset

| Index | CGPA  | Placed (yᵢ) |
|:-----:|:-----:|:-----------:|
| 0     | 5.70  | 0           |
| 1     | 6.25  | 1           |
| 2     | 7.10  | 0           |
| 3     | 8.15  | 1           |
| 4     | 9.60  | 1           |

---

# 🧠 2. Initial Prediction Setup

- **Positive class proportion**:
  \[
  p = \frac{3}{5} = 0.6
  \]
- **Log-odds (F₀)**:
  \[
  \log\left(\frac{0.6}{0.4}\right) \approx 0.405
  \]
- Thus:
  - Initial prediction for every sample = **0.6 probability**
  - Hessian for each point ≈ \( p(1-p) = 0.6 \times 0.4 = 0.24 \)

---

# 🧩 3. Data Table with Predicted Probability and Residuals

| Index | CGPA  | Placed (yᵢ) | Predicted (pᵢ) | Residual (rᵢ = yᵢ - pᵢ) |
|:-----:|:-----:|:-----------:|:--------------:|:-----------------------:|
| 0     | 5.70  | 0           | 0.6            | -0.6                    |
| 1     | 6.25  | 1           | 0.6            | +0.4                    |
| 2     | 7.10  | 0           | 0.6            | -0.6                    |
| 3     | 8.15  | 1           | 0.6            | +0.4                    |
| 4     | 9.60  | 1           | 0.6            | +0.4                    |

✅ Now you can clearly see how **Actual (yᵢ)** and **Predicted (pᵢ)** create the **Residuals (rᵢ)**.

---

# 🛠️ 4. Possible Splits on CGPA

Sorted CGPA values:

| Between | Threshold (Candidate Split) |
|:-------:|:----------------------------:|
| 5.70 and 6.25 | 5.975 |
| 6.25 and 7.10 | 6.675 |
| 7.10 and 8.15 | 7.625 |
| 8.15 and 9.60 | 8.875 |

---

# 🧮 5. Calculate Gain at Each Split

### Gain formula:

\[
\text{Gain} = \frac{G_L^2}{H_L+\lambda} + \frac{G_R^2}{H_R+\lambda} - \frac{(G_L+G_R)^2}{H_L+H_R+\lambda}
\]

where:
- \( G_L, G_R \) = sum of residuals left/right
- \( H_L, H_R \) = sum of hessians left/right
- \( \lambda = 1 \) (regularization)

---

### 🔹 Split at CGPA = 5.975

| Side | CGPA | Residuals rᵢ | G | H |
|:----:|:----:|:------------:|:-:|:-:|
| Left | 5.70 | -0.6         | -0.6 | 0.24 |
| Right| 6.25, 7.10, 8.15, 9.60 | +0.4, -0.6, +0.4, +0.4 | 0.6 | 0.96 |

**Gain:**

\[
= \frac{(-0.6)^2}{1.24} + \frac{(0.6)^2}{1.96}
= 0.2903 + 0.1837
= 0.474
\]

✅ Gain = **0.474**

---

### 🔹 Split at CGPA = 6.675

| Side | CGPA | Residuals rᵢ | G | H |
|:----:|:----:|:------------:|:-:|:-:|
| Left | 5.70, 6.25 | -0.6, +0.4 | -0.2 | 0.48 |
| Right| 7.10, 8.15, 9.60 | -0.6, +0.4, +0.4 | 0.2 | 0.72 |

**Gain:**

\[
= \frac{(-0.2)^2}{1.48} + \frac{(0.2)^2}{1.72}
= 0.0270 + 0.0233
= 0.0503
\]

✅ Gain = **0.0503**

---

### 🔹 Split at CGPA = 7.625

| Side | CGPA | Residuals rᵢ | G | H |
|:----:|:----:|:------------:|:-:|:-:|
| Left | 5.70, 6.25, 7.10 | -0.6, +0.4, -0.6 | -0.8 | 0.72 |
| Right| 8.15, 9.60 | +0.4, +0.4 | 0.8 | 0.48 |

**Gain:**

\[
= \frac{(-0.8)^2}{1.72} + \frac{(0.8)^2}{1.48}
= 0.3721 + 0.4324
= 0.8045
\]

✅ Gain = **0.8045** ✅ (BEST)

---

### 🔹 Split at CGPA = 8.875

| Side | CGPA | Residuals rᵢ | G | H |
|:----:|:----:|:------------:|:-:|:-:|
| Left | 5.70, 6.25, 7.10, 8.15 | -0.6, +0.4, -0.6, +0.4 | -0.4 | 0.96 |
| Right| 9.60 | +0.4 | 0.4 | 0.24 |

**Gain:**

\[
= \frac{(-0.4)^2}{1.96} + \frac{(0.4)^2}{1.24}
= 0.0816 + 0.1290
= 0.2106
\]

✅ Gain = **0.2106**

---

# 📊 6. Final Summary Table

| Split CGPA | Gain    |
|:----------:|:-------:|
| 5.975      | 0.474   |
| 6.675      | 0.0503  |
| 7.625      | **0.8045** ✅ (Best) |
| 8.875      | 0.2106  |

🔔 **Best split is at CGPA = 7.625**

---

# 🌳 7. Decision Tree Diagram

```plaintext
                      (All Samples)
                            |
                CGPA <= 7.625 ? (Best Split)
                  /                     \
    (Yes) Lower CGPA               (No) Higher CGPA
(5.70, 6.25, 7.10)             (8.15, 9.60)

(Avg residual = -0.8/3)       (Avg residual = +0.8/2)
(predicted shift)
```

In reality:
- Each leaf would **adjust** its prediction slightly based on residual averages.
- After this split, predictions become **better aligned** with actual placements!

---

# 🎯 Steps followed to determine the right split

| Step | Action |
|:----:|:------:|
| 1 | Predict initial probability (0.6) for all |
| 2 | Compute residuals (yᵢ - pᵢ) |
| 3 | Try all CGPA splits |
| 4 | Compute Gain for each |
| 5 | Pick split with maximum Gain |
| 6 | Create Tree Split at CGPA = 7.625 |
